About Star Pets

Star Pets started with a simple idea: food and accessories good enough that your dog or cat notices the difference. We pick every recipe and product ourselves, and we keep the range small on purpose so everything on the shelf is something we would feed our own pets.

Based locally and run by a small team, we handle orders, packing and delivery ourselves, so if something isn't right, a real person fixes it.

Questions about an order or a product? Reach out any time and we will get back to you.
